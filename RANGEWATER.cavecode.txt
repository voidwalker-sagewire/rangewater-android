🪨 BLOCK 4 — SETTLED DECISIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. RangeWater is a native Android application built with Kotlin and Compose.
2. The intended package identity is com.sagewire.rangewater.
3. The application is offline-first and will use MapLibre rather than Google Maps.
4. Turf is a direct compile dependency for geodesic circle calculations.
5. The baseline grazing visualization uses a geodesic radius of 243.84 meters
   (800 feet) around a water asset.
6. Terrain barriers and slope will eventually constrain the simple radius.

EVIDENCE STATUS:
- Jim Gerrish, Management-intensive Grazing (2004), University of Missouri FSRC
  materials, and the USDA-NRCS National Range and Pasture Handbook were named in
  RW-TX-001-REV2 as supporting sources.
- Exact pages and passages supporting the behavioral 800-foot interpretation have
  not yet been preserved in this repository.
- Until that evidence audit is completed, 800 feet is the ratified project design
  baseline, not a fully verified scientific claim.

🌐 BLOCK 9 — PUBLIC-SAFE DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RangeWater is an offline-capable Android utility for cattle producers and rotational
grazers. It maps grazing-distance radii around water assets to help producers plan
trough placement, fence layouts, and pasture utilization.
