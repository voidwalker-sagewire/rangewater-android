RangeWater helps cattle producers and rotational grazing managers understand how pasture water placement affects grazing access.

Draw pasture boundaries, place water sources, and see the land within the preferred 800-foot planning distance. RangeWater also shows the 800-to-1,000-foot transition band and the acreage beyond planned coverage, giving you a practical view of where another water point may help.

FIELD MAPPING
• Draw and edit pasture boundaries over aerial imagery
• Add water sources and assign them to accessible pastures
• Add gates directly to fence segments
• Preserve shared fence corners when boundaries change

WATER COVERAGE
• View preferred, transition, and beyond-planned coverage
• Compare covered acreage and percentages for each pasture
• Switch between physical-radius and accessible-pasture views

HERD MOVEMENT
• Create herds and record their current location
• Plan movement through mapped gates
• Complete or cancel movements while preserving history

LOCAL-FIRST DATA SAFETY
• Ranch records remain on your device
• Create portable, checksummed RangeWater backup files
• Restore records on another Android phone or tablet
• Undo the most recent restore when an earlier archive was selected by mistake

RangeWater is a planning aid. Its map estimates do not replace an on-site inspection, survey, engineering analysis, veterinary guidance, or professional grazing plan.
